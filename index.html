Privacy Policy — Wardex

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Thank you for using Wardex ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is stored, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Overview

Wardex is a personal wardrobe-management app. A core design principle is on-device, local-first storage: your wardrobe data and photos are stored on your own device and are not uploaded to any server operated by us. We do not run a cloud backend, and we do not collect, sell, or monetize your data.

2. Information the App Handles

The App may handle the following types of information, all of which you provide by using the App:

  • Account information — a username and password you create locally for multi-user separation. This is stored only on your device.
  • Wardrobe content — photos of clothing items, categories, colors, styles, materials, tags, storage locations, and any notes you add.
  • Outfit information — the combinations of clothing items you assemble and save.
  • Other in-app records — calendar/wear logs and budget/ledger entries you create.
  • Usage preferences — app settings and your last-used account, stored locally.

3. Camera and Photo Library

To add clothing items, the App may request access to:

  • Camera — to take a new photo of a clothing item.
  • Photo Library — to select an existing photo from your device.

These permissions are used only for the purpose you initiate (adding or editing clothing). The App does not access your camera or photo library in the background. You may change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

4. On-Device AI Processing (Subject Cutout)

The App offers an optional "subject cutout" feature that isolates a clothing item from a photo. This processing uses on-device machine-learning frameworks provided by Apple (such as Vision / VisionKit). Images are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

5. How Your Data Is Stored

Photos are stored in the App's private sandbox directory on your device. Text metadata (clothing records, outfits, tags, account information) is stored in the App's local storage on your device.

Your data remains on your device. It is not synced to, or backed up by, any server we control. If you delete the App from your device, the App's locally stored data may be removed.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising services. Because your data is stored locally, we have no access to your wardrobe content or photos.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained on your device for as long as you use the App. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account. Deleting an account permanently removes that account's wardrobe records, photos, outfits, and related information from your device. This action cannot be undone.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App stores data locally, we do not have access to any user's data regardless of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: 13510810815@139.com.

隐私政策 — Wardex

最后更新日期：2026年11月16日

感谢您使用 Wardex（以下简称“本应用”）。我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明本应用会处理哪些信息、如何存储这些信息，以及您拥有的选择权。使用本应用即表示您同意本政策所述的做法。

1. 概述

Wardex 是一款个人衣橱管理应用。其核心设计原则是设备端、本地优先存储：您的衣橱数据和照片仅存储在您自己的设备上，不会上传至我们运营的任何服务器。我们没有云后端，不会收集、出售或将您的数据变现。

2. 本应用处理的信息

本应用可能会处理以下您在使用过程中提供的信息：

  • 账户信息——您为了区分多用户而在本地创建的用户名和密码。这些信息仅存储在您的设备上。
  • 衣橱内容——衣物照片、类别、颜色、款式、材质、标签、收纳位置以及您添加的任何备注。
  • 穿搭信息——您搭配并保存的衣物组合。
  • 其他应用内记录——您创建的日历/穿着记录以及预算/账目条目。
  • 使用偏好——应用设置以及您最近使用的账户，均存储在本地。

3. 相机与相册

为了添加衣物，本应用可能会请求访问以下权限：

  • 相机——用于拍摄衣物的新照片。
  • 相册——用于从您的设备中选择现有的照片。

这些权限仅用于您主动发起的操作（添加或编辑衣物）。本应用不会在后台访问您的相机或相册。您可以随时在设备的“设置”中更改或撤销这些权限。

4. 设备端 AI 处理（主体抠图）

本应用提供可选的“主体抠图”功能，用于将衣物从照片中分离出来。该处理使用 Apple 提供的设备端机器学习框架（如 Vision / VisionKit）。图像完全在您的设备上处理，绝不会传输给我们或任何第三方。

5. 数据存储方式

照片存储在您设备上本应用的私有沙盒目录中。文本元数据（衣物记录、穿搭、标签、账户信息）存储在您设备的本地存储中。

您的数据保留在您的设备上。它不会同步或备份到我们控制的任何服务器。如果您从设备中删除本应用，应用本地存储的数据可能会被移除。

6. 数据共享

我们不会向第三方出售、出租或共享您的信息。我们不使用第三方分析或广告服务。由于您的数据存储在本地，我们无法访问您的衣橱内容或照片。

7. 数据保留与删除

只要您使用本应用，您的数据就会保留在您的设备上。您可以随时通过“设置 → 删除账户”来删除账户。删除账户将永久从您的设备上移除该账户的衣橱记录、照片、穿搭及相关信息。此操作无法撤销。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，我们不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。由于本应用在本地存储数据，无论用户年龄大小，我们都无法访问任何用户的数据。

9. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何更改都将通过更新本页面顶部的“最后更新日期”来体现。我们建议您定期查看本政策。
